The idea

Good secondhand pieces sell badly when they're photographed poorly and priced on guesswork. Thrift Bae fixes both. Every piece is sourced by hand, researched against what similar items actually sold for, styled, shot, and listed with honest notes on condition and fit.

It's a one-woman operation, run end-to-end by its founder — sourcing, pricing, photography, content, and customer care.

How it works

01

Sourced by hand

Pieces are picked one by one for fabric, condition, and that NYC street-style feel — never bulk-bought by the pound.

02

Priced from real sales

Each price is set just under recent sold listings for the same kind of piece — fair for the buyer, honest for the seller.

03

Listed with honesty

Real photos, real measurements, and flaws stated up front. What you see is exactly what arrives.
